# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

This is sample policy content for the Project Shonku HR Policies knowledge
base.

- Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PTO per calendar year, credited
  monthly at 1.5 days/month.
- Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next year;
  anything beyond that is forfeited on January 1st.
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance through
  the HR portal for approval by your manager.
- Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and is not capped.
