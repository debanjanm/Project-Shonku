# Remote Work Policy

This is sample policy content for the Project Shonku HR Policies knowledge
base.

- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default; fully remote
  arrangements require director-level approval.
- Remote workdays must overlap with core hours, 10am-4pm in the employee's
  local timezone, for meeting availability.
- The company provides a one-time $500 home office stipend for remote
  employees, covering desk, chair, or monitor purchases.
- Equipment issued for remote work remains company property and must be
  returned upon separation.
